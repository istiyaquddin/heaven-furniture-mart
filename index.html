The Journey

Milestones of Craftsmanship

From our founding workshop to nationwide recognition as one of Bangladesh's premier bespoke furniture ateliers.

2020

Founded in Chattogram

Founded by Managing Director Abul Kalam Bhuiyan with a mission to bring true bespoke luxury and architectural craft to Chattogram homes.

01
Genesis

Established our initial woodworking atelier focusing exclusively on custom client commissions.

2021

Agrabad Flagship Showroom

Opened our expansive physical showroom along Agrabad Access Road opposite RAK Ceramics, creating a curated interior studio destination.

02
Expansion

Enabled clients to touch premium fabrics, inspect joinery, and consult with in-house designers in person.

2024–2025

International Furniture Fair

Prominently exhibited signature bespoke collections at the International Furniture Fair, Chattogram to acclaim from leading architects and developers.

03
Exhibition

Showcased curved velvet suites and solid teak architectural dining masterpieces.

2025

Chamber of Commerce Induction

Formally inducted as an esteemed member of the Chattogram Chamber of Commerce & Industry (CCCI), reinforcing trade excellence.

04
Prestige

Strengthened institutional partnerships for corporate and commercial bespoke fit-outs.

2026

BFIOA Nationwide Recognition

Awarded prestigious nationwide recognition from the Bangladesh Furniture Industry Owners Association (BFIOA) for artisanal mastery.

★
National Honor

Solidified status as one of Bangladesh's premier luxury bespoke furniture brands.
